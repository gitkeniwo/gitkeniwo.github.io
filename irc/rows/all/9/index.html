  • 0000[#lbxd]keniwo
    watched Solaris (1972) [↗] ♥ ★★★★★

    if you just finished the book and would like to watch a verbatim movie adaption, tarkovsky's version is definitely not for you. It is, however, a typical tarkovsky masterpiece in that one could only be amazed at how he explores the human emotions and consciousness in a both poetic and scifi way.

    Harley's rather eccentric behaviors are well explained in the novel, but when scrutinized in the film's setting, it strikes the audience as chaotic and bizarre. Only when you give it another thought can you figure out what kind of horrible ordeal she was suffering before her final disappearance. I know some people might not enjoy tarkovsky's approach to the story, but such tricks really add a touch of style and uniqueness to the film.
